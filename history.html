HISTORICAL JOURNEY

Tracing the Story of Lepakshi

Lepakshi is closely associated with the Vijayanagara period and is celebrated for its temple architecture and sculpture.

01

Ancient Sacred Landscape

Lepakshi developed as a sacred landscape containing shrines associated with Shiva, Vishnu and Veerabhadra.

02

Vijayanagara Tradition

The present temple complex reflects the distinctive architectural and artistic tradition of the Vijayanagara period.

03

Virupanna & Veeranna

Traditional accounts and district tourism records associate the development of the present temple with the brothers Virupanna and Veeranna.

04

The Temple Complex

Pillars, sculptures, mandapas and painted surfaces transformed the site into an extraordinary heritage complex.

05

Lepakshi Today

Today the site attracts pilgrims, history enthusiasts, architecture students, photographers and travellers.

LEGENDS

Stories Connected With Lepakshi

Lepakshi is associated with several traditional legends. One famous tradition connects the place with Jatayu from the Ramayana.

The name Lepakshi is popularly connected with the Telugu expression “Le Pakshi”, interpreted in the traditional story as “Rise, bird”.

These stories form part of the cultural memory surrounding the heritage site and should be understood as traditional legends rather than independently verified historical facts.
