SACRED HERITAGE

Veerabhadra Temple

A remarkable expression of Vijayanagara temple architecture.

THE TEMPLE

A Monument of Stone & Devotion

The Veerabhadra Temple is the principal monument of Lepakshi and an important heritage attraction in Andhra Pradesh.

01

Temple Complex

The temple complex contains shrines, halls, pillars, sculptures and other architectural elements.

02

Stone Craftsmanship

Detailed stone carving is one of the defining visual characteristics of the temple.

03

Mandapas

Pillared halls create spaces filled with sculpture, ornamentation and architectural detail.

04

Painted Art

The temple is also celebrated for its historic paintings and decorative artwork.

ARCHITECTURAL MARVEL

The Famous Hanging Pillar

Among the many pillars of the temple complex, the Hanging Pillar is particularly famous because it appears to remain suspended above the floor.

The feature demonstrates the sophistication of stone construction and has become one of Lepakshi's most recognizable attractions.
