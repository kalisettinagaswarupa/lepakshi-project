GETTING THERE

How to Reach Lepakshi

Lepakshi is located in Sri Sathya Sai district of Andhra Pradesh, close to Hindupur.

🚗

By Road

Lepakshi is well connected by road and is approximately 15 km from Hindupur according to district tourism information.

🚆

By Rail

Hindupur is a useful nearby railway connection. Check current railway schedules before travelling.

✈️

By Air

Bengaluru's Kempegowda International Airport is a major airport option for visitors approaching the region.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Lepakshi located?

Lepakshi is in Sri Sathya Sai district of Andhra Pradesh, near Hindupur.

What is Lepakshi famous for?

It is particularly known for the Veerabhadra Temple, Monolithic Nandi, Hanging Pillar, sculptures and historic artwork.

What should I see first?

The Veerabhadra Temple is the main heritage attraction, followed by the Monolithic Nandi and other architectural features.

Are current timings and ticket prices listed here?

No. Visitor rules, opening hours and ticket information can change, so check current official information before travelling.
