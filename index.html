THE HERITAGE

An Architectural Story Carved in Stone

Lepakshi is associated with the Vijayanagara tradition of architecture. Its temple complex brings together elaborate pillars, sculptures, carvings and painted surfaces.

The official district tourism information identifies the Veerabhadra Temple as an important example of Vijayanagara architecture and highlights its Hanging Pillar and monumental Nandi.
